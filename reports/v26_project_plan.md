# V26 CalibSDEdit project plan

V26 freezes the useful V25 raw-support deterministic and population anchors, replaces the coordinate-unstable learned-basis residual target with a 46-channel sensor-space artifact target, and compares a moderate-noise deterministic-warm-start SDEdit refiner against a capacity-matched one-step refiner.

The matched one-step comparison is a competitive positioning and mechanism analysis, not a retention gate for diffusion. Natural artifact attenuation together with neural preservation has higher interpretive priority than a strict `diffusion > deterministic` ordering on paired point metrics.

The work is development-only. Test inference is EEG plus query-disjoint support only; query EOG, query operator, and event data remain evaluator-only after output freeze. All confirmation and sealed sources remain unopened. The manuscript and A-track remain unchanged.
