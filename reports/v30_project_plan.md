Hard boundaries are checkpoint/provenance parity, support/query separation, same-noise context intervention, output freeze before natural evaluator access, participant-first aggregation, zero sealed reads, and no changes to historical model/training sources. Scientific magnitude and confidence intervals inform a human candidate selection; they are not automatic gates.
