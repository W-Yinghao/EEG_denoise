# Scope A manuscript blueprint

1. Introduction — narrowed question and withdrawn original claims.
2. Related Work — EEG diffusion, calibration, falsification, privacy.
3. Query-Disjoint Support-Conditioned Diffusion — frozen clean-room candidates and inference boundary.
4. Evaluation Protocol and Falsification Controls — common panel, all donors, lag/shuffle/null, participant-first statistics.
5. Paired Utility and Correct-Context Specificity — absolute metrics before contrasts.
6. Natural Attenuation–Retention Trade-off — no physiological-preservation alias.
7. Computational Cost and Support Burden — V31 exact durations and V30 step/latency data.
8. Privacy-Linkage Audit — linkage risk without anonymity claims.
9. Limitations and Claim Boundary — within-session, fixed montage, no task-valid physiology or confirmation.
10. Conclusion — viable but mixed evidence; no deployment claim.
