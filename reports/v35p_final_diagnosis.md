# V35P final diagnosis

## Final positioning

```text
C. Fiber-Stratified-Resample is clearly preferable
```

The exact-fiber privacy result survives, but the diffusion-specific empirical claim
does not. Head-aware accounting gives Fiber-OneStep, Fiber-Stratified-Resample, and
Fiber-SANDiff the same strong endpoint boundary and the same primary adaptive
finite-threat value once A_H is included (`0.579090`). No registered conditional
fiber leakage is detected beyond H.

Compared with Fiber-SANDiff, the simple stochastic baseline is better on energy
distance, MMD, variance calibration, and secondary retrained utility. Fiber-SANDiff
is better only on the registered covariance discrepancy and produces no exact
training-fiber duplicates. That evidence is insufficient to retain a unique
diffusion-specific empirical advantage.

The positive method result is therefore narrowed to exact function-preserving
population fiber replacement. Fiber-SANDiff remains a learned/amortized implementation,
but empirical superiority over simple resampling is withdrawn. No new method family
should be introduced on BCI-IV-2a.

This result does not establish anonymity, mutual-information estimation, removal
below the H-visible boundary, cross-dataset generalization, or clinical use. Latency
was not measured, used, or considered in the decision.
