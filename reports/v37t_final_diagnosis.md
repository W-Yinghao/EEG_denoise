## Frozen waveform method

V27 EnergySDEdit-L0.5 remains a positive, attenuation-oriented development method: paired temporal
RRMSE is `0.747098`; natural EOG remaining ratio is `0.929094` (absolute attenuation `1.618737 dB`);
low-EOG observation retention is `0.807347`; PSD and covariance distortions are `0.336428` and
`0.240253`. It is slightly worse than matched EnergyDET on paired RRMSE and is not a universally
safe physiological restorer.

Support-conditioned paired value is retained, but existing all-donor/falsification evidence makes
matched-donor specificity heterogeneous and non-unique. V31 exact support-duration rows supersede
the invalid V30 duration contract.

## Stochastic evidence

K=16 draws are severely under-dispersed. L0.5 80% coverage is `0.002866`, the interval score does not
beat a matched-width constant reference, error–dispersion rho is `-0.044502`, and the sample mean
does not materially improve point fidelity. Stochastic variance is preferentially aligned with the
support projector, but this mechanism-level structure is not sufficient for calibrated or useful
uncertainty.

## Final positioning

**B — Method-centric trade-off, uncertainty exploratory.** The TAAS revision can present frozen
CalibEnergy-SDEdit-L0.5 as a transparent waveform denoising operating point with absolute natural
attenuation and an explicit observation-retention trade-off. It must not retain the proposed
“empirically useful uncertainty” clause, claim unique operator recovery, physiological preservation,
formal safety, or diffusion superiority over matched deterministic estimation.

No new method family is authorized by this diagnosis. The next action is manuscript/reviewer
integration of the frozen evidence, without opening sealed data in V37T.
