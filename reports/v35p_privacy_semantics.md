# V35P privacy semantics

For every strong exact-fiber endpoint, the released representation has the form

\[
Z'=z_H(H)+NG(H,\xi),
\]

where `G` receives centered logits and independent population randomness but not
the source fiber, source subject, or test support. Because centered logits are also
recoverable from `Z'`, the correct theoretical endpoint is

\[
I(Z';S\mid Y)=I(H;S\mid Y).
\]

Accordingly, V35P does not interpret differences among finite attacks on strong
Fiber-OneStep, Fiber-Stratified-Resample, and Fiber-SANDiff as differences in ideal
information removal. Primary registered finite leakage is the maximum of attacks
using H, Z, and [H,Z]; it cannot be reported below the A_H result.

The session-separated cross-entropy difference `CE(A_H)-CE(A_HU)` is only a closure
diagnostic. It is negative for all three strong channels with both attacker families,
so the registered attackers find no additional subject predictability from released
fiber coordinates after H is explicit. This is not a conditional-mutual-information
estimate or an anonymity result.

For weak and medium deterministic interpolation, source U remains algebraically
recoverable when alpha is below one. Historical finite-attacker curves therefore do
not establish information removal away from the strong endpoint.
