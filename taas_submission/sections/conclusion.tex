\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Early same-session subject calibration improves a fixed EOG-guided diffusion
EEG denoiser on held-out later EEG relative to population and mismatched-subject
conditioning. Across nine BCI2b development participants and three training
seeds, matching reduces paired RRMSE by $0.03473$ versus population and by
$0.04836$ versus the frozen cyclic unseen-subject operator; all participants
have the same direction for both effects. Mean natural-signal proxy criteria
were met, with one preservation and two covariance participant exceptions.

The conclusion is deliberately narrow. Query EOG is required. The three seeds
measure optimization stability on the same development cohort, not an
independent-cohort evaluation. The diffusion estimator does not beat either the matched
deterministic network or linear regression. The current evidence therefore
supports matching-subject conditioning within a fixed diffusion pipeline, not
diffusion-specific personalization, uniform safety, or general EEG artifact
removal. Independent cohorts and broader acquisition settings remain necessary.
