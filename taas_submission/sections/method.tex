\section{Subject-Calibrated EOG-Guided Residual Diffusion}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Setting and Information Boundary}
Let $s$ denote a held-out participant and let $\mathcal S_s$ be an early
same-session calibration segment containing synchronized EEG and EOG. A later
query window contains observed EEG $\mathbf y\in\mathbb R^{C\times T}$ and EOG
$\mathbf e\in\mathbb R^{E\times T}$. Both are deployment inputs. Participant
identity, query clean EEG, motor-imagery labels, artifact annotations, and a
query-fitted transfer are unavailable to the denoiser.

For paired training and evaluation only, we write
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf y=\mathbf x+\mathbf a,
  \label{eq:paired_observation}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf x$ is a low-ocular-activity EEG carrier and $\mathbf a$ is an
ocular component constructed from disjoint real EEG/EOG time ranges. This is
real-signal-backed semi-simulation, not natural clean ground truth. Natural EEG
is evaluated separately.

\subsection{Support-Derived Contamination Operators}
After robust, outer-training normalization, ridge regression on $\mathcal S_s$
estimates the matched transfer
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf H_s=\mathbf Y_s\mathbf E_s^\top
  (\mathbf E_s\mathbf E_s^\top+\lambda\mathbf I)^{-1},
  \label{eq:operator}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf Y_s$ and $\mathbf E_s$ concatenate support EEG and EOG samples.
The ridge setting and normalization are fixed without query outcomes. The
population operator $\mathbf H_0$ is an equal-subject estimate from the seven
outer-training participants. The frozen two-heldout split excludes both the
recipient and its cyclic wrong donor; consequently this is not a standard
eight-subject leave-one-out population model. The primary wrong control
$\mathbf H_d$ comes from that compatible cyclic donor, which is unseen during
population training.

For a context $c\in\{s,0,d\}$, the linear artifact anchor and output are
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf a_{0,c}=\mathbf H_c\mathbf e,
  \qquad
  \widehat{\mathbf x}_{\mathrm{lin},c}
  =\mathbf y-\gamma_s\mathbf a_{0,c}.
  \label{eq:linear_anchor}
\end{equation}
The scalar $\gamma_s$ is fixed from support only and shared across MATCH, POP,
and WRONG. Thus the operator comparison does not use query outcomes to tune
correction strength.

\subsection{Deterministic Residual Estimator}
The linear operator is imperfect. A one-step network $f_\phi$ predicts the
normalized remaining artifact
\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\mathbf r}_{\mathrm{det},c}
  = f_\phi(\mathbf y,\mathbf e,\mathbf a_{0,c}).
  \label{eq:det_residual}
\end{equation}
Let $\boldsymbol\sigma$ be a robust per-channel scale estimated on outer-training
artifact residuals. The deterministic correction is
\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\mathbf a}_{\mathrm{det},c}
  =\mathbf a_{0,c}+\boldsymbol\sigma\odot
  \widehat{\mathbf r}_{\mathrm{det},c}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Diffusion of the Remaining Artifact Residual}
Diffusion models only the normalized artifact residual left after the linear and
deterministic estimates. On a paired training row, its target is
\begin{equation}
  \boldsymbol\delta_c^*=
  (\mathbf a-\mathbf a_{0,c})\oslash\boldsymbol\sigma
  -\widehat{\mathbf r}_{\mathrm{det},c}.
\end{equation}
Under a cosine schedule, the forward process is
\begin{equation}
  \boldsymbol\delta_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\boldsymbol\delta_c^*
  +\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\boldsymbol\epsilon,
  \qquad \boldsymbol\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(\mathbf0,\mathbf I).
\end{equation}
A one-dimensional U-Net predicts the $v$ parameterization conditioned on
$(\mathbf y,\mathbf e,\mathbf a_{0,c},\widehat{\mathbf r}_{\mathrm{det},c})$.
We use EMA weights, DDIM with 25 reverse steps, and $K=8$ posterior streams.
The point estimate is their arithmetic mean,
$\overline{\boldsymbol\delta}_c=K^{-1}\sum_k
\widehat{\boldsymbol\delta}^{(k)}_c$; no sample is selected by an outcome.
The complete diffusion correction and reconstructed EEG are
\begin{align}
  \widehat{\mathbf a}_{\mathrm{diff},c}
    &=\mathbf a_{0,c}+\boldsymbol\sigma\odot
      (\widehat{\mathbf r}_{\mathrm{det},c}+
       \overline{\boldsymbol\delta}_c),\\
  \widehat{\mathbf x}_{\mathrm{diff},c}
    &=\mathbf y-\gamma_s\widehat{\mathbf a}_{\mathrm{diff},c}.
  \label{eq:reconstruction}
\end{align}

\subsection{Context Interventions and Randomness}
DIFF-MATCH, DIFF-POP, and DIFF-WRONG differ only through $\mathbf H_c$ and the
quantities downstream of it. They share model weights, normalization, query,
$\gamma_s$, and the window-level $K=8$ noise bank. The cyclic unseen-WRONG is
the primary specificity control. Compatible donors that were seen during
population training are reported separately as a donor sensitivity analysis;
they are not additional unseen replications. A temporal-shuffle control destroys
the order of query EOG while retaining its amplitude distribution.
