\section{Seed Stability and Donor Roles}
\label{app:seed_donor}

The mean $(U_P,U_W)$ pairs for seeds 20260808, 20260810, and 20260811 are
$(0.03654,0.04667)$, $(0.03465,0.04779)$, and $(0.03299,0.05062)$.
Each effect is positive for 9/9 participants at every seed. These 27
participant--seed comparisons are not independent; the participant-averaged
effects are the inputs to the exact sign-flip tests.

For recipient $s\in\{1,\ldots,9\}$, the frozen primary wrong donor is
$s+1$ with wraparound. Both recipient and donor are excluded from population
training. Each fold therefore contains one unseen wrong donor and seven donors
that were seen during population training. The latter are a donor sensitivity,
not unseen-donor replication.

\section{Safety Distribution}
\label{app:safety}

The participant minima/maxima complement the means in the main paper:
preservation has mean/minimum $0.7982/0.7618$, covariance distortion has
mean/maximum $0.1237/0.1660$, EOG attenuation has mean/minimum
$0.1043/0.0653$, and MI-band PSD distortion has mean/maximum
$0.0719/0.0954$. The minimum participant MI-kappa difference relative to
DIFF-POP is $-0.0199$. These distributions motivate reporting mean proxy
criteria together with every participant-level exception.

\section{Reproducibility Boundary}
\label{app:reproducibility}

All scientific tables are reconstructed from committed participant-level
summaries. Checkpoints, prepared arrays, per-window outputs, raw data, and logs
remain server-side. The evidence manifest records the source summary for every
number and figure without modifying the frozen scientific CSV or JSON files.
