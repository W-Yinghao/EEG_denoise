\title[The Operator Carries the Subject]{The Operator Carries the Subject: Subject-Aware EEG Denoising by Calibrated Artifact Operators over a Population Diffusion Prior}

\author{Anonymous Authors}
\affiliation{%

\begin{abstract}
Ocular artifacts contaminate electroencephalography (EEG) differently in every person, because the propagation of eye potentials to the scalp depends on anatomy and electrode placement. Denoising networks trained on a population absorb this variation into shared parameters and cannot state what should be removed for a new user. We make a population diffusion model subject-aware at inference time. The model, trained once, represents the structure of clean EEG; a two-minute calibration segment recorded from each user provides a closed-form estimate of that user's electrooculography (EOG) to EEG propagation matrix, stabilized by empirical Bayes shrinkage toward the population estimate; the propagated EOG then guides the sampler as an explicit estimate of the ocular contribution, and the operator's posterior uncertainty is sampled jointly with the diffusion trajectories. The result is a complete adaptation loop with a measured behavior at every stage: sixty seconds of calibration already buys 97\% of the two-minute gain, the reliability criterion withholds the guide when calibration fails instead of substituting a population estimate, the calibrated operator has a measurable lifetime that points the loop at recalibration, and the sampler reports its remaining uncertainty as predictive intervals. On paired data built from real ambulatory EEG and EOG, subject-calibrated guidance improved reconstruction for every development participant and for seven of eight held-out participants evaluated once under a frozen protocol. On natural recordings it is the only method in our comparison, classical anchors included, that removes substantially more ocular signal than a population baseline while keeping low-artifact segments intact. The predictive intervals reach nominal 80\% and 90\% coverage on the development cohort and hold at 0.81 and 0.86 on the held-out cohort under a temperature frozen in advance, and the same cleaning preserves downstream brain--computer interface decoding on the dataset's native tasks.
\end{abstract}
