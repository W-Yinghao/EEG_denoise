# CGDR Eye-BCI ME outer fold 00

Evidence status: `exploratory_pre_repair_not_gate_evidence`.

This directory records one held-out-participant natural-EEG engineering run
for S01/Sess01/ME011. It has no paired clean waveform target and reports only
external-reference attenuation and observation-relative preservation proxies.
It does not provide the participant/session count, cross-session drift,
physiological non-inferiority, learned attenuation, strong baselines, or
frozen statistics required by formal G1--G5. Every formal gate is therefore
**NOT RUN/BLOCKED**. The numbers are retained for debugging and design only,
not as gate or manuscript evidence.
